KAOMA POLICY CENTRE UPDATE

Upload every file and folder from this ZIP to the root of the GRV / KAOMA GitHub repository. Commit the upload and wait for the production deployment to show Ready.

AFTER DEPLOYMENT
1. Open the KAOMA admin dashboard.
2. Open Photos, Logo & Homepage.
3. Complete these new fields:
   - Legal business / owner name
   - Complete business postal address with PIN code
   - Customer-support phone with country code
   - Grievance contact name and email
4. Confirm the support email and click Save storefront settings.
5. Open www.kaoma.in and press Ctrl + Shift + R once.
6. Open every footer policy link and verify the business details.

POLICIES INCLUDED
- Worldwide Shipping and Delivery
- Customs and Duties
- Returns, Refunds and Cancellations
- Privacy Policy
- Terms and Conditions
- Adults-only and Responsible Shopping Policy
- Discreet Packaging Policy
- Contact and Grievance Support

IMPORTANT
These pages are a practical store-policy starting point and not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer or tax/compliance professional. Complete real business details and review the policies before enabling live payments.
