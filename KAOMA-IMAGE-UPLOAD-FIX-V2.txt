KAOMA ADMIN IMAGE UPLOAD FIX V2

1. Upload all three code files from this ZIP to the GRV KAOMA GitHub repository, preserving their folders.
2. Wait until the Vercel production deployment shows Ready.
3. Open https://kaoma.in/admin-login in an Incognito window and sign in again once.
4. Open Products & Inventory and select one JPG, PNG or WebP main image.
5. Wait until its preview and the message "Main product image uploaded" appear.
6. Select the additional images and wait until their previews appear.
7. Click Add product or Save changes to save the product information.

FIXES INCLUDED
- Main and additional images upload immediately after selection.
- The main-image preview appears before the product is saved.
- Additional images unlock after the main image finishes.
- Expired admin sessions renew automatically.
- File-specific type and size errors are displayed.

Accepted images: JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP. Convert HEIC/HEIF to JPG first.
